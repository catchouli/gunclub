    Questions

    Frequently asked

    Do I need a licence to shoot here?

    Not to get started. Air guns at the club don't need a certificate, and probationary members can shoot club rifles under supervision. You'll only need a firearm certificate if you want to own your own rifle, and the club can help with that once you're a member. Wording to be checked by the committee.

    I've never shot before. Is that OK?

    Absolutely. Most members started as complete beginners. Experienced members and range officers will take you through safety and technique, and air rifle is a great place to start.

    What age can I join?

    Minimum age and junior arrangements: TBC. We regularly host Scout groups for competitions.

    What should I bring?

    Just yourself. Club guns are free to borrow, and you pay for pellets or ammunition as you use them. Hearing/eye protection and ID requirements: TBC.

    Can I bring a friend?

    Look out for guest days in the diary, which are the best way for friends and family to have a go. Guest rules: TBC.

    Is the club accessible?

    Step-free access, parking and toilets: TBC. We've hosted groups of shooters with disabilities and are happy to talk through what you need.
